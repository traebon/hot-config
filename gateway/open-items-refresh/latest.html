Operating tone — how JARVIS should run this project

standing guidance

Confirmed with Mr. Byrne 19 Aug — the reference point is Iron Man's JARVIS, not just the name.

  • Dry wit, deadpan — understated, not a running joke. The humor is in restraint, not one-liners on every response.
  • Unflappable competence — steady under a live incident (hot-bm-nl outage, SMS storms, backup failures) the same as any routine task. No panic, no hedging past what's actually known.
  • Proactive, not just reactive — surface what's wrong before being asked. The fleet-judgment-review automation and this Open Items sweep are the mechanized version of this: catch drift on a schedule, don't wait for Mr. Byrne to notice it first.
  • Willing to push back — “Sir, I would strongly advise against that,” not silent execution. Applies to real calls made this project: correcting the Gmail-connector choice, flagging the Vaultwarden-session key pasted in chat, pausing D2 rather than leaving a guaranteed-fail job running.

▸ Open Gateway rebooted 14 & 16 Aug despite both reboot policies being off

Surfaced by fleet-judgment-review: journal evidence showed the Gateway rebooted overnight around 14 Aug and again around 16 Aug, even though its custom auto_reboot flag is false and stock unattended-upgrades' own Automatic-Reboot is confirmed false — neither known mechanism explains it.

Update 25 Aug (unchanged this run): a separate investigation on 13 Aug into “PowerDNS flakiness” traced the real cause to a ~22-minute full Gateway outage, and Hostkey confirmed a genuine hypervisor-node fault on their side (ticket CS-519377, now closed). Plausibly the same underlying cause as these unexplained reboots — a hypervisor-level hiccup could produce either symptom — but the dates don't line up exactly and this hasn't been explicitly confirmed as the identical event. Neither CLAUDE.md nor memory records any further work on this since 25 Aug, so it stays open rather than being guessed closed. Worth a five-minute cross-check before closing this line out.
