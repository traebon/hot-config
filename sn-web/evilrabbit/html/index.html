About the studio

One artist, one weird rabbit, no committee.

Evil Rabbit Art started as a sketchbook character and grew into a full studio practice — paintings, prints, and merch, all built and shipped from the same small operation. If it's on the site, it's real work, not stock art.

New pieces drop first on Instagram and TikTok before they ever hit the shop — follow along to see work in progress, not just the finished piece.
